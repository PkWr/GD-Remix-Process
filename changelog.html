Changelog

Spot

What’s been added, newest first

23 October 2026

  • Your own images. Choose a folder or some photos, or drop them onto the picture. Nothing is uploaded. Chrome and Edge remember the folder for next time.
  • Apply changes straight away (N): each change redraws the picture on screen at once, for demoing.
  • About in the bottom bar, with new Privacy and visit-count notes (GoatCounter: no cookies, no tracking).
  • Quieter bottom bar: a small pill that fades when idle.
  • Info panel beside Controls, with its own close; draggable on desktop.
  • Text forms sketch: typed text as 3D forms (separate page, an experiment).

21 October 2026

  • Opens on Patrick Walker’s own images, from Concrete, Foxes and Schwalbe.
  • 3D forms wrap seamlessly from one edge of the screen to the other.
  • With the camera on, the picture holds still.

17 October 2026

  • House style: IBM Plex Mono italic with a yellow accent.
  • Start screen: an open circle that becomes a window onto the motion, and a side strip with About.
  • About page, including energy and sustainability.
  • An opening look for first visits.

16 October 2026

  • Named Spot (Spot Room on the Igloo walls, Spot Reel for the showreel).
  • Glass surface for 3D forms: they magnify and bend the picture behind.
  • Bottom bar on desktop as well as phones; screen size picked to suit the device.

15 October 2026

  • 3D forms: pictures wrapped round shapes that move through the space.
  • Showreel: a sequence of looks with its own play bar.

11–12 October 2026

  • New controls panel with tabs, cards and a phone-friendly layout.
  • Move: pan, tilt and zoom, with loop and strip modes and strip layers.
  • Echo trail behind moving pictures.
  • Scenes: save looks and share them by link or QR code.
  • Igloo test page for staff.

10 October 2026

  • Igloo Immersive Suite support: six-wall stage and a panning preview.
  • Live camera through the effects.
  • Layouts that adapt to the screen’s resolution; touch controls for phones.

1 October 2026

  • Desktop screen size and an Igloo check page.

July 2026

  • First version, for the outreach workshop on 10 July: students’ photos processed live on the wall with duotone, pixelate, halftone, crossfades and blends, a control panel, an image uploader and offline images in case the wifi failed.
