Spot

A generative photo-remix tool by Patrick Walker

Photographs remixed live in the browser: halftone, pixel, ASCII and duotone, moving strips with echo trails, and 3D forms that wear the images or bend them like glass. It runs on a phone, on a laptop and across the six walls of an immersive room.
