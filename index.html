Spot

A generative photo-remix tool by Patrick Walker

Part of GD v/x AI, a Scholarship of Teaching and Learning project at Sheffield Hallam University, asking whether AI supplants creativity in graphic design, or co-enables and augments it.

Spot is motion tooling that runs in a web browser: no specialist hardware, no account, no subscription, and nothing uploaded. The same tooling scales from a phone to a desktop to the six walls of the Igloo Immersive Suite, in real time, with no render pass.

The AI is in the making, not the running: every effect is a drawing technique, not a generative model.
